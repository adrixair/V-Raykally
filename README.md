## Your first render, in four steps

1. **Install V-Raykally** using the download for your platform.
2. **Connect V-Ray Standalone.** Check the detected executable in Settings, or select it manually.
3. **Add your scenes.** Import `.vrscene` or `.vrs` files and drag jobs into the order you want.
4. **Press Play.** Follow progress, inspect logs, and adjust or retry jobs when needed.

> **Before you start:** a working V-Ray Standalone installation and a valid Chaos/V-Ray license are required. V-Raykally is free; the renderer and its license are separate.

## Local by design

Render files and queue processing stay on your computer. AI diagnostics run locally. Optional app analytics are enabled only with your consent; see the [Privacy Policy](https://v-raykally.adrienlejeune.com/privacy/) for details.
